Privacy Policy

Anuva Cleaning Corporation is committed to protecting the privacy and security of the personal information we collect from clients, prospective clients, vendors, and website visitors.

1. Information We Collect

We may collect personal information that you provide directly to us, including your name, company name, email address, phone number, mailing address, project details, and other information needed to provide cleaning or facility services.

We may also collect information automatically through our website, including browser type, device information, IP address, pages viewed, and referral source. This helps us understand how visitors use our site and improve performance and user experience.

2. How We Use Information

We use the information we collect to respond to inquiries, provide quotes and service information, manage contracts and project coordination, communicate with clients and vendors, improve our website and services, and comply with legal and contractual obligations.

3. Sharing of Information

We do not sell personal information. We may share information with trusted service providers that assist with business operations, communication, hosting, analytics, or legal compliance, subject to confidentiality obligations.

We may also disclose personal information when required by law, regulation, legal process, or to protect the rights, safety, or property of Anuva Cleaning Corporation or others.

4. Cookies and Website Analytics

Our website may use cookies or similar technologies to improve user experience, analyze website traffic, and understand visitor behavior. You may configure your browser to reject or delete cookies, though some website functions may be limited as a result.

5. Data Security

We employ reasonable administrative, technical, and organizational measures designed to protect personal information from unauthorized access, use, or disclosure. However, no system can be guaranteed to be completely secure, and we cannot guarantee the security of information transmitted over the internet.

6. Retention

We retain personal information for as long as necessary to fulfill the purposes described in this policy, comply with legal obligations, resolve disputes, and enforce agreements.

7. Your Rights

Depending on your jurisdiction, you may have rights regarding access, correction, deletion, restriction of processing, or objection to certain uses of your personal information. To exercise these rights, please contact us using the information below.
